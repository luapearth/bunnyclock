A few things to know

Little questions.
Clear answers.

For the grown-ups behind the smiles.

Does it need internet?+

Attendance works fully offline, and face recognition runs on the device. Pro is a Google Play subscription.

Where are faces stored?+

Face prints are stored only on the tablet and aren’t included in device backups. Sending them over Bluetooth is off by default and must be switched on by an adult. CSV exports never contain face prints.

Can a photo fool it?+

BunnyClock uses a quick liveness check—a blink, smile or head turn—so a photo can’t clock someone in. BunnyClock is a friendly helper for classroom routines, not a security or access-control system.

Which devices?+

Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or later.

How do I cancel Pro?+

Cancel anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Pro is a monthly subscription and renews until you cancel.

Is there an iPhone version?+

Not yet.
