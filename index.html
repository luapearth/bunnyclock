A few things to know

Little questions.
Clear answers.

For the grown-ups behind the smiles.

Does it need internet?+

Attendance works fully offline, and face recognition runs on the device. Basic and Pro are Google Play subscriptions; the app checks your plan with Google Play when it’s online and keeps working offline for up to 15 days.

Where are faces stored?+

Face prints are stored only on the phone or tablet and aren’t included in device backups. Only Pro devices can send them over Bluetooth, and only when an adult switches it on—it’s off by default. CSV exports never contain face prints.

Can a photo fool it?+

BunnyClock uses a quick liveness check—a blink, smile or head turn—so a photo can’t clock someone in. BunnyClock is a friendly helper for classroom routines, not a security or access-control system.

What are subjects?+

With Basic or Pro, teachers who teach more than one class create subjects, such as 📐 Math or 🔬 Science, and pick one under the big button before class. Every clock-in and clock-out is linked to it, each subject has its own IN and OUT, and records can be moved to another subject later.

Can I import my class list?+

Yes, with Pro: import a spreadsheet (CSV) with name, student ID and class, or a BunnyClock export. You see a preview before anything changes. New students then register their face.

Which devices?+

Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or later.

Is there a free trial?+

Basic has a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You can cancel before it ends in Google Play and you won’t be charged.

How do I cancel a subscription?+

Cancel Basic or Pro anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Both are monthly subscriptions and renew until you cancel.

Is there an iPhone version?+

Not yet.
