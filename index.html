How it works

Three seconds at the tablet, and the child is on their way.

  1. 1

    Look at the bunny

    The child puts their face inside the bunny’s face — a camera frame shaped like a bunny, with an oval cut-out.

  2. 2
    🙂
    • Blink 😉
    • Smile 😄
    • Turn ↔

    Blink, smile or turn

    A quick liveness check, so a photo can’t clock anyone in.

  3. 3

    “Hi, Mia!”

    A big screen greets the child by name with confetti. At home time the same bunny says “Bye, Mia!”. The first scan of the day is IN, the next is OUT.

Questions teachers ask

Does it need internet?

No. BunnyClock works fully offline — face recognition runs on the device. Google Play needs a connection when you buy or cancel a Pro subscription.

Where are faces stored?

Only on the tablet, in the app’s private storage. BunnyClock keeps a face print — a short list of numbers calculated from the face — and never saves the camera image. Records aren’t included in device backups.

Can a photo fool it?

No. A quick liveness check — blink, smile or turn the head — happens before a face can clock anyone in.

Which devices does it work on?

Android 8.0 and later, on phones and tablets.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is a monthly Google Play subscription. Cancel anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions, and it renews until you do.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet.
