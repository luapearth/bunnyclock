BunnyClock for Android

Our privacy policy.

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · App: BunnyClock for Android (com.luapearth.bunnyclock)

The short version

  • BunnyClock works offline. We don’t run servers, and we don’t collect, see or sell any of your data.
  • Student names, face prints and attendance records are stored only on your device.
  • Data leaves the device only when a grown-up chooses to send it by Bluetooth or share it as a spreadsheet.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

Who this policy is for

BunnyClock is an attendance app for classrooms and families. It is set up and managed by a teacher, school or parent (“you”), and used by young students to clock in and out. This policy explains what the app stores, where it stays and what control you have. If you use BunnyClock at a school, the school decides how the app is used and is responsible for getting any consent its local laws require, including consent for face recognition.

What the app stores on your device

Data Why Where
Student name, school student ID, class and chosen animal buddy To list students and match records across your classroom devices On the device only
Face prints: a short list of numbers calculated from the student’s face To recognise the student when they clock in or out On the device only
Attendance records: clock-in, clock-out and missed clock-out times, with a match score To show records and daily attendance On the device only
Settings, such as theme colors and an optional background photo you choose To personalise the app On the device only

Camera images are not saved. The front camera is used live to find and recognise a face. Each camera frame is processed in memory and discarded; BunnyClock keeps only the face print, which cannot be turned back into a photo.

All of this is kept in the app’s private storage on the device and is not included in device backups.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it does not send any student information, face prints or records to us or to anyone else. We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

When data leaves the device

Only when a grown-up chooses to, and only in these two ways. Both are protected by a grown-ups-only question so children can’t start them.

  • Bluetooth transfer to another device you have paired with, such as the teacher’s tablet. You choose which students and which day to send. Names and attendance records are sent; face prints are sent only if you switch on “Also send faces”, which is off by default.
  • Share records as a spreadsheet (CSV) through Android’s share menu, to an app you pick (for example email or Google Drive). The spreadsheet contains names, student IDs, classes, clock times and match scores. It never contains face prints. Once shared, the file is handled by the app you sent it to.

Cloud sync is not available in the app.

Services provided by Google

  • Face detection (Google ML Kit). BunnyClock uses Google’s ML Kit library, which runs entirely on the device, to find faces in the camera view. Camera images and face data are not sent to Google. The library may send Google limited diagnostic information, such as device model, Android version, app version and performance metrics. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Purchases (Google Play). BunnyClock Pro is a subscription bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; the app only receives whether Pro is active. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Camera: to recognise faces when registering a student and when clocking in or out.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): only for Bluetooth transfer between paired devices.
  • Internet: used by Google Play for purchases. BunnyClock itself does not send your data over the internet.

Children

BunnyClock is made for use with young children under the supervision of a teacher or parent. We do not collect personal information from children. Purchases, sharing and transfers are behind a grown-ups-only question. There are no ads and no links out of the app for children to follow.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Deleting a student in the app removes their details, face prints and attendance records from that device.
  • Uninstalling BunnyClock removes all of its data from the device.
  • Records you have already sent by Bluetooth or shared as a spreadsheet stay wherever you sent them; delete them there too if needed.

Because we don’t hold any of your data, we can’t view, export or delete it for you. Everything is under your control on the device.

Security

Data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps can’t read, and is protected by your device’s screen lock and encryption. Keep classroom devices locked when not in use.

Changes to this policy

If BunnyClock changes how it handles data, for example by adding a cloud feature, we will update this page and the effective date before that change reaches the app.
